Escalation Guidelines

↗
When to call in staff support:
🔒 Security & Abuse
Hacked sites, spam, malware, or Terms of Service violations
💳 Billing Issues
Refunds, payment disputes, subscription problems, or upgrade issues
🌐 Domain Problems
Domain transfers, DNS issues, or domain registration problems
⚙️ Technical Issues
Site downtime, database errors, or platform bugs beyond volunteer scope
👤 Account Access
Locked accounts, ownership disputes, or account recovery issues
📧 Email Services
G Suite/Titan setup issues, email delivery problems, or mailbox access
⚖️ Legal Matters
DMCA requests, copyright issues, or legal compliance questions
