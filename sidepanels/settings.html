About This Extension

Version: 1.0.2
Purpose: Support tools for WordPress.com volunteers

This extension provides quick access to support resources, common responses, escalation guidelines, and note-taking capabilities. All sections are customizable and can be rearranged to fit your workflow.

Features

  • Support Search: Quickly search WordPress.com support documentation
  • YouTube Search: Find official WordPress tutorials and videos
  • Bookmarks: Customizable quick links to frequently used pages
  • Common Responses: Pre-written responses for frequent questions
  • Site Profiler: Analyze WordPress.com sites
  • Status Pages: Monitor Automattic service status
  • Common Issues: Quick reference for troubleshooting
  • Escalation Guidelines: Know when to call in staff support
  • Quick Notes: Take and manage notes with timestamps
